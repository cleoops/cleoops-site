**What the Memo Actually Says**

Key findings from the memo:

1. **Maven Smart System dependency**: "Maven Smart System currently processes 3,000 target candidates per day using Claude-derived inference. Alternative systems have not been validated for this volume or complexity."

2. **Research disruption**: "Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory has integrated Claude into daily workflows for 10,000+ scientists. Removal would require re-architecture of research pipelines that typically take 6-12 months to migrate."

3. **Operational continuity**: "Combatant commands in US Central Command and US Indo-Pacific Command have developed operational procedures assuming Claude availability. Removal would require rewriting decision trees and re-validation of mission-critical systems."

4. **No validated alternative**: "No alternative system has been approved for classified use that matches Claude's performance on the required workloads. Validation of alternative systems would require 12-18 months minimum."

5. **Cost implications**: "System re-architecture and alternative validation estimated at $2-4 billion and 12-18 months."

This is the Pentagon's own analysis of why removing Claude is operationally infeasible.

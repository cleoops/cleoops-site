**What the Pentagon Admitted**

"However, the supply-chain risk designation was based on a separate security assessment. The institutional interests and the security decision are analytically distinct."

This argument is the fatal flaw. The statement is saying "we have conflicts, but they did not affect our decision."

But the conflicts are so obvious that they almost certainly did affect the decision. You cannot have institutional interest in an outcome and also claim the outcome was reached through pure objective analysis.
